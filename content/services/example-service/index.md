## Precision Casting Service

Industrial Manufacturer is a **precision casting service** provider that builds components to your drawing rather than pulling a standard shape from a catalog. When an off-the-shelf part will not do — because the geometry is proprietary, the alloy is specific, or the volume does not justify a mass-market tool — precision casting lets us make exactly the part your design calls for. We help engineering teams move from a CAD model and a specification to a finished, certified component, whether that is a single prototype or a recurring production order.

## Introduction

Precision casting exists for the parts that machining, fabrication or forging struggle with: organic contours, integrally cast features, thin walls, internal passages and consolidated assemblies. By pouring metal into a fine ceramic shell built around your pattern, we capture that complexity as a single near-net-shape component.

For procurement teams, precision casting is about control. You define the material, the tolerance and the finish; we deliver to it. For design engineers, it means freedom to optimize a part for function rather than for what a stock process can conveniently make.

## Production Process

The sequence begins with pattern production from your tooling, followed by building a ceramic shell layer by layer. After the shell is cured, the pattern is melted out and molten metal is poured into the cavity. Once cooled, the shell is removed, the casting is cut from its runner, CNC machined to drawing, finished and inspected.

## Tolerance Capability

Dimensional accuracy is where precision casting earns its keep. As-cast features hold tight tolerance grades, and features that must mate with other parts — sealing faces, bores, threads and locating surfaces — are brought to tighter limits through CNC machining.

## Quality Control

Quality is engineered in, not inspected in. Our program includes:

1. **Heat chemistry verification** before pour.
2. **Dimensional inspection** on coordinate measuring machines against the CAD model.
3. **Non-destructive testing** where the application demands it.
4. **Traceability** — material certificates, dimensional reports and process records travel with each order.
