## Surface Treatment & Finishing Services

Industrial Manufacturer provides **surface treatment and finishing** that protects components and meets appearance, wear and corrosion requirements. Passivation, anodizing, powder coating and plating are applied in-house, so a raw or machined part leaves as a finished, specification-compliant component.

## Introduction

Finish is often what determines a part's service life and first impression. By keeping finishing in-house alongside our other processes, we control preparation, thickness and adhesion rather than handing that responsibility to a third party.

## Manufacturing Capability

| Finish | Benefit |
|--------|--------|
| Passivation | Enhanced corrosion resistance on stainless steel. |
| Anodizing | Hard, decorative, insulating layer on aluminum. |
| Powder Coating | Durable, uniform color and protection. |
| Plating | Wear, corrosion or conductivity properties. |

## Tolerance & Thickness Capability

We control coating thickness to avoid interfering with mating features and verify it against the drawing. Adhesion and appearance are checked before release.

## Quality Control

1. **Surface preparation** verified before finishing.
2. **Thickness measurement** per specification.
3. **Adhesion / appearance checks** on samples.
4. **Traceable records** with each order.

## Industries Served

## Design for Manufacturability

We review finish selection against the service environment and part geometry, and advise on masking and tolerances so the finish performs as intended.
