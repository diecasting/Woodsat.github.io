## Sheet Metal Fabrication Services

Industrial Manufacturer delivers **sheet metal fabrication** for enclosures, brackets, panels and structural frames. Laser cutting, press braking and welding are managed as one integrated workflow, so a flat pattern becomes a finished, weld-inspected assembly without leaving our facility.

## Introduction

Fabrication complements our casting and machining capabilities, letting us supply everything from simple brackets to complex welded assemblies. Programmed tooling and consistent setups keep dimensions stable from the first part to the last.

## Manufacturing Capability

| Process | Capability |
|---------|-----------|
| Laser Cutting | Accurate, burr-free profiles from flat sheet. |
| Bending | Press braking to tight bend angles and radii. |
| Welding | MIG/TIG welding of frames and assemblies. |
| Finishing | Deburr, clean and prepare for coating. |
| Inspection | Dimensional and weld-quality checks. |

## Tolerance Capability

Laser cutting and programmed bending hold consistent profiles and bend geometry. Weld fixtures keep assemblies square and dimensionally stable.

## Quality Control

1. **First-article check** of cut and bent samples.
2. **Weld inspection** for penetration and appearance.
3. **Final dimensional review** against drawings.
4. **Clean and package** for safe delivery.
