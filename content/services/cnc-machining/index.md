## CNC Machining Services

Industrial Manufacturer provides **CNC machining** that turns rough castings, forgings and bar stock into finished, inspection-ready components. Multi-axis machining centers and CNC lathes let us hold tight, repeatable tolerances on the features that matter — sealing faces, bores, threads and locating surfaces — in a single, well-controlled setup.

## Introduction

Machining is where cast and forged components become functional parts. By combining in-house CNC capability with our casting and fabrication processes, we eliminate hand-offs between suppliers and keep lead times short and results consistent.

## Manufacturing Capability

| Process | Capability |
|---------|-----------|
| Milling | 3-, 4- and 5-axis machining of complex geometries. |
| Turning | CNC lathes for shafts, bushings and rotational parts. |
| Drilling/Tapping | Precision holes, threads and cross-holes. |
| Inspection | In-process checks and final CMM verification. |

## Tolerance Capability

We bring critical features to tight limits through CNC machining and verify them on coordinate measuring machines. Stable processes keep variation low across production runs.

## Quality Control

1. **In-process checks** at setup and during runs.
2. **Final CMM inspection** against the CAD model.
3. **First-article approval** before volume production.
4. **Traceable documentation** with every order.
