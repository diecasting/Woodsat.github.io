## Assembly & Value-Added Services

Industrial Manufacturer turns individual components into **finished, inspection-ready modules** through sub-assembly, kitting, functional testing and packaging. Consolidating build and test under one roof reduces your supplier count and shortens the path from parts to a shippable product.

## Introduction

Many programs stall not on making parts but on putting them together reliably. Our assembly cells combine components from our own casting, machining and fabrication lines into tested units, with the documentation procurement teams need.

## Quality Control

1. **Build instructions** confirmed before start.
2. **In-process checks** at key join points.
3. **Functional testing** with recorded results.
4. **Traceable build records** per order.
