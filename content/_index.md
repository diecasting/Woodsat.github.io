## A Trusted Wooden Speaker Cabinet Manufacturing Partner

Woodsat is a **custom wooden speaker cabinet manufacturer** serving audio brands, OEMs and ODM programs around the world. We combine engineering, CNC wood routing, veneer craftsmanship, finishing and acoustic quality control under one roof so that accountability for sound and build quality stays with a single partner. From a single prototype to high-volume production runs, our team helps audio companies move from a concept and a specification to a finished, inspection-ready cabinet.

## Why Choose Woodsat

- **Single-source manufacturing** — engineering, CNC routing, veneering, finishing and acoustic QC in one facility.
- **Engineering support** — design-for-manufacturability reviews before any tooling is cut.
- **Acoustic focus** — bracing, damping and port geometry tuned to driver sets, not just boxes.
- **Brand matching** — veneer book-matching and piano-lacquer finishes that look premium in showrooms.
- **Global supply** — documented packing and logistics aligned with international requirements.

## Our Capabilities

Woodsat builds wooden speaker cabinets across the full range of audio products: bookshelf and floor-standing hi-fi enclosures, studio monitors, subwoofers, soundbars, commercial and installed audio, and custom OEM enclosures. We machine panels, baffles, ports and joinery on multi-axis CNC wood routing centers, then dress them with veneer or paint and verify them acoustically before shipping.
