Each step is documented against your specification: patterns are checked against master gauges, shell thickness is controlled by slurry layers, and pour temperature is logged for every heat.

## Quality at Every Stage

1. **Pattern control** — verified against master gauges.
2. **Shell control** — layer count and composition logged.
3. **Pour control** — temperature recorded per heat.
4. **Final inspection** — CMM and NDT where required.
