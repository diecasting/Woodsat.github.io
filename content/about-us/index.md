## About Woodsat

Woodsat is a **custom wooden speaker cabinet manufacturer** built for audio brands that care about how their products sound and look. We combine acoustic engineering, CNC wood routing, veneer craftsmanship, finishing and quality control in one facility, so the cabinet you approve is the cabinet that ships — at any volume.

## What We Do

We manufacture wooden speaker cabinets and acoustic enclosures across the audio spectrum: bookshelf and floor-standing hi-fi, studio monitors, subwoofers, soundbars, commercial and installed audio, and custom OEM enclosures. Every project moves through engineering, machining, veneering, finishing, acoustic measurement and assembly under one roof.

## How We Work

- **Engineering first** — design-for-manufacturability reviews before tooling.
- **Confidential programs** — NDA-backed OEM and ODM development with controlled documentation.
- **Acoustic discipline** — bracing, damping and port tuning validated per project.
- **Consistent quality** — serialized traceability and pair-matched measurement.

## Our Capabilities

From a single prototype to high-volume annual runs, Woodsat applies the same process control. Multi-axis CNC routing, book-matched veneer, piano-lacquer finishing and acoustic QC give brands a cabinet that performs and presents at reference level.
