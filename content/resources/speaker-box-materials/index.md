## Speaker Box Materials

Speaker box materials define how a cabinet behaves under driver load. The ideal panel is stiff, dense and well-damped so it does not add its own coloration. Woodsat works with the full range of wooden materials and helps brands choose the right one for sound, look and cost.

## Material Comparison

| Material | Stiffness | Resonance | Finish | Best for |
|----------|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| MDF | Medium | Low | Paints best | Painted & gloss cabinets |
| Baltic Birch Plywood | High | Low-medium | Exposed edge | Pro & portable audio |
| Solid Hardwood | High | Variable | Premium | Reference hi-fi |
| HDF | High | Low | Mirror gloss | High-gloss painted |

## Composites and Laminates

Constrained-layer and resin-composite panels are used where extra damping is needed, often as baffles or bracing rather than full panels, to tame specific resonance modes.

## Matching Material to Product

A small bookshelf speaker and a touring subwoofer have different priorities. We select material, thickness and bracing together so the cabinet meets both acoustic and mechanical needs without overspending.
