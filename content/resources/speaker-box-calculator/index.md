## Speaker Box Calculator

A speaker box calculator turns driver specifications into enclosure dimensions. It is the starting point for any design, but the numbers it returns still need manufacturing and acoustic review to become a real, great-sounding cabinet.

## What the Calculator Needs

- **Driver parameters** — Vas, Qts, Fs and Xmax from the datasheet.
- **Target alignment** — sealed, vented (ported), bandpass or transmission line.
- **Desired tuning** — the port resonance frequency for vented boxes.

## What It Returns

The calculator outputs internal volume, port diameter and port length, and sometimes baffle layout. These define the air spring and resonance that shape bass response.

## Why Manufacturing Review Still Matters

Calculated volume ignores panel thickness, bracing volume, driver displacement and internal damping — all of which shift the real tuning. Woodsat validates the design, adjusts for construction, and confirms the result by measuring finished cabinets.
