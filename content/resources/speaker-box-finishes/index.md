The finish is where a wooden speaker cabinet becomes a product. Woodsat offers a full range of speaker box finishes — from deep high-gloss piano lacquer to understated matte and durable textured coatings — applied in filtered spray rooms for a flawless surface.

## High-Gloss Piano Lacquer

Piano lacquer is built from many thin coats, sanded and polished to a mirror finish. It is the premium look for showpiece hi-fi cabinets, but it demands a perfect substrate — usually MDF or HDF — and careful process control.

## Matte and Satin

Matte and satin finishes reduce glare and fingerprints and suit minimalist or studio environments. They are more forgiving of minor surface imperfections while still reading as refined.

## Textured and Soft-Touch

Textured coatings add grip and conceal handling marks, useful for portable and pro-audio products where durability matters more than showroom shine.

## Choosing a Finish

Consider brand positioning, the cabinet material, and how the product will be handled. A veneered baffle with a painted body is a common way to combine luxury grain with a consistent colored shell.
