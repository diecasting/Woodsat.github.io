## Subwoofer Enclosure Design

Subwoofer enclosure design is where bass is won or lost. The box tunes the driver's low-frequency output, controls excursion and determines how clean and powerful the bottom end feels. Woodsat builds wooden subwoofer cabinets for all common alignments.

## Common Alignments

- **Sealed** — tight, accurate bass with gentle roll-off; simplest and most robust.
- **Ported** — louder, deeper bass at the tuning frequency; needs careful port design to avoid chuffing.
- **Bandpass** — high output over a narrow band; common in car and installed audio.
- **Transmission line** — extended, articulate low end; complex and large.

## Building a Low-Resonance Sub

Subwoofers move a lot of air, so panels must be stiff and well-braced to avoid buzz. We use dense panels, internal bracing grids, and measured damping so the cabinet stays quiet under high excursion.

## Port Design

Port area, length and shape set the tuning and control air noise. Woodsat machines ports to match the calculated tuning while keeping velocity low for clean output.
