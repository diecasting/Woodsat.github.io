## Wooden vs MDF Speaker Cabinets

The wooden vs MDF speaker cabinet debate comes down to what your product needs to communicate and how it needs to perform. Both are "wood" in the broad sense — MDF is a wood-fibre panel — but they behave differently in the workshop and in the listening room.

## Acoustic Behavior

MDF's uniform density gives consistent, low-resonance panels that are easy to model and repeat. Veneered or solid wooden cabinets can look more luxurious but introduce grain-direction variables that must be managed with bracing and damping.

## Finish and Appearance

This is where wooden cabinets shine. A book-matched veneer or solid hardwood reads as craftsmanship and commands a higher shelf price. MDF, by contrast, is the better substrate for painted high-gloss lacquer.

## Strength and Durability

Plywood and solid wood resist impact and racking better than MDF, which is brittle at edges. For portable or pro-audio use, a layered wooden structure is often the safer choice.

## Cost and Production

MDF is cost-effective and machines predictably, which helps tight tolerances at volume. Exotic veneers and solid wood raise both material and labor cost.
