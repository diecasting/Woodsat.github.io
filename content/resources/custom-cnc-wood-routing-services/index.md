## Custom CNC Wood Routing Services

Our custom CNC wood routing services turn panel layouts into precise, repeatable parts. Multi-axis routers cut baffles, port tubes, rebates, dowel holes and joinery so that every cabinet assembles square and tight, run after run.

## What We Machine

- **Baffles** with driver cutouts, countersinks and terminal cavities.
- **Panels** with edge profiles, rebates and locating dowels.
- **Ports** as shaped tubes or slots tuned to target tuning frequency.
- **Internal bracing** cut as interlocking grids for stiffness.

## Why Tight Tolerances Matter

A cabinet that is 0.5 mm out across many panels accumulates into gaps, rattles and inconsistent acoustic volume. CNC control eliminates that drift, so left and right pairs match and assembly is fast and clean.

## From Prototype to Volume

We cut prototypes on the same machines and tooling used for production, so what you approve is what ships. Tool libraries, fixturing and programs are version-controlled per project.
