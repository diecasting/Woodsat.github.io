## Casting Tolerance Guide

This guide explains how to specify dimensions for manufactured components so the result matches your intent. Good tolerance specification reduces cost, avoids unnecessary machining and prevents fit problems at assembly.

## As-Cast vs Machined

As-cast features are economical but hold wider limits. Features that must mate with other parts — sealing faces, bores, threads and locating surfaces — are brought to tighter limits through CNC machining.

## Specifying Dimensions

1. Mark only the features that truly need tight limits as machined.
2. Reference international standards so inspection maps to your drawing.
3. Keep wall thickness and draft angles manufacturing-friendly.
