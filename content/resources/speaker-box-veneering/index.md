## Speaker Box Veneering

Speaker box veneering is the craft of applying a thin wood veneer to a stable substrate so the cabinet looks like solid wood at a fraction of the cost and weight. Done well, it is indistinguishable from solid timber and far more stable.

## Why Veneer

Solid wood moves with humidity and is expensive in wide widths. Veneer lets you wrap large, flat baffles in continuous grain while keeping the panel dimensionally stable — essential for acoustic consistency.

## Book-Matching vs Slip-Matching

- **Book-matching** opens sequential leaves like a book so the grain mirrors across the center — the classic premium look.
- **Slip-matching** places leaves side by side for a repeating, calmer pattern.

## Edge Treatment

The exposed edge can be a matching veneer band, a solid wood lipping, or a painted reveal. A clean edge is what separates a budget box from a reference cabinet.

## Finishing Veneer

Veneer takes the same lacquer, matte and satin finishes as solid wood, and book-matched grain under high-gloss lacquer is among the most striking looks in hi-fi.
